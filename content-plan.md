WHY THIS ANGLE WINS FOR A SOLO BLOG
- Long-tail, conversational keywords convert ~2.5x better than generic ones
  (Digistore24 data: 1-word = 0.17% conversion, 4-word = 1.61%+).
- Big sites (TechRadar, etc.) write for pros and rank broad terms ("best AI tools").
  A small blog wins the SPECIFIC long-tail: "best free AI tool for X" / "AI for
  normal people who Y". Target those.
- Sweet spot for a new site: 500–1,000 monthly searches, keyword difficulty < 35,
  clear buyer intent. Avoid head terms.
