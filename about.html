Why this site exists

Written by Ali Maher

Most AI content is written by tech people, for tech people. If you're not in tech, you're expected to decode "LLM," "context window," and "agentic workflow" before you're allowed to pick a tool. That's backwards.

AI for Real Life is for everyone else. Every guide answers one question: which tool do I use for this specific job, and what does it cost? Plain English. Real limits. Honest comparisons. No hype, no 40-link roundups.

How I test: I use each tool's free tier for real tasks — writing, research, images, math — for at least a week before I put it in a guide. If a free limit changes, the guide gets updated. That's the deal.

How I make money: some links are affiliate links. When you buy through them, the vendor pays me and your price doesn't change. Affiliate links never change what I say — if a free tool is the right answer, that's what I'll tell you.
